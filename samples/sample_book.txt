The Book Reader

Chapter One

Reading is a conversation. All books talk. But a good book listens as well.

The habit of reading is one of the greatest resources of mankind. It is a quiet pleasure that stays with you long after the last page is turned.

Chapter Two

Words have power. When you save a word to your vocabulary, you give it a place to live in your memory. Select any word, right-click, and choose "Save to Vocabulary."

Highlight your favorite passages in yellow, green, blue, or pink. Your highlights are saved automatically and restored when you reopen the book.

When you close the app or open another book, your reading position is remembered. Press Ctrl+B to save your position manually, or Ctrl+Shift+B to jump back to it.

Happy reading!
